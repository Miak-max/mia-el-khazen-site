About me

Bridging the gap between spatial architecture and live experience, I am a Paris-based designer creating immersive environments for music, fashion, and live performance.

With a background in architecture, my practice centers on translating sound, narrative, and brand identity into tangible physical worlds ranging from festival stages and intimate concert sets to fashion campaigns. Operating out of my Paris atelier, I combine conceptual spatial design with hands-on fabrication, prototyping and building much of what I design under one roof to ensure every detail translates seamlessly from drawing to production.

Services: Set & Production Design | Spatial Concepts | Fashion Campaign Art Direction
